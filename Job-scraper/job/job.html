How to Apply for ONGC Apprentice Recruitment  ?

 
Interested and eligible candidates may now apply online for these vacancy by visiting official ONGC Recruitment Portal. Candidates may follow below mentioned steps to apply for this recruitment.

# Scroll down, go to Important Web-Links section .

# Click on the "Online Application Form" and a new webpage will be opened.

# Fill your all personal and educational details and upload necessary documents and submit the form.

# At the end, take a print out of the application form .

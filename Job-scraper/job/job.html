Other Criteria of NEEPCO Apprentice Recruitment


Resident-ship: The candidate should be a resident of North East India.

Qualification Date:

# For Graduate Engineering & Diploma Engineering, the candidate should have completed his / her Graduation / Diploma not before 31st March,2024.

# For Non-Engineering Graduate or equivalent in general streams such as B.A, B.Sc & B.Com, the candidate should have completed his / her Graduation / Diploma not before 31st March,2024.

# For ITI Trade Apprentices, the candidate should have completed ITI course.

Id Proof: The candidate must have Aadhaar Card (compulsory) / Pan Card/ Voter ID Card/ Passport which is required for online registration.

Age Limit: Maximum age 28 years as on 01-09-2025, but not less than 18 years.

Age Relaxation: Age relaxation is allowed for Scheduled Caste/ Scheduled Tribe/ Other Backward Classes/ Persons with Disability/ Ex-servicemen, as per applicable Govt. of India rules.

Reservation: Reservation for Scheduled Caste/ Scheduled Tribe/ Other Backward Classes (NCL)/ Persons with Disability/ Economically Weaker Section, will be as per existing Govt. of India rules.
 

How to Apply for NEEPCO Recruitment ?


(A) For Graduate Engineering & Diploma Engineering

➢ Candidates must apply online at National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) and no other mode of application will be accepted.

Step I
➢ The Candidates must get himself / herself enrolled / registered with Board of Practical Training (BOPT), through online enrollment at National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS), in https://nats.education.gov.in/ and posses a valid enrollment ID generated after successful enrollment.
➢ For instance, there may be candidates who possess qualification of both B.Tech and Diploma. If he/ she apply against Technician (Diploma) training slots, he/she must register as Diploma only in the NATS portal.

Step II
➢ After successful online registration at in https://nats.education.gov.in/ and receipt of a valid enrollment ID, the candidate is advised to log in using your registered ID and Password can apply against the mentioned Establishment of NEEPCO and opt for the desired stream in line with fulfillment of educational qualifications and eligibility criteria.

(B) For Non-Engineering Graduate or equivalent in general streams such as B.A, B.Sc & B.Com

➢ Candidates must apply online at National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS) and no other mode of application will be accepted.

Step I
➢ The Candidates must get himself / herself enrolled / registered with Board of Practical Training (BOPT), through online enrollment at National Apprenticeship Training Scheme (NATS), in https://nats.education.gov.in/ and poses a valid enrollment ID generated after successful enrollment.

Step II
➢ After successful online registration at https://nats.education.gov.in/ and receipt of a valid enrollment ID, the candidate is advised to log in using your registered ID and Password and apply against any one of the mentioned Establishment of NEEPCO and opt for the desired stream in line with fulfillment of educational qualifications and eligibility criteria.

(C) ITI Trade Apprentice (Electrician, Lineman, Plumber & Fitter)

➢ Candidates must apply online at National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) (www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) and no other mode of application will be accepted.

Step
➢ The Candidates must get himself / herself enrolled / registered with National Promotion Scheme (NAPS), through online enrollment at www.apprenticeshipindia.gov.in and poses a valid enrollment ID generated after successful enrollment.
